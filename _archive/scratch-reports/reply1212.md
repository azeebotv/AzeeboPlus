# Azeebo+ Project Brief: UI/UX Design Guidance

---

### 1. What is the main purpose of the Azeebo+ website?
Azeebo+ is **not just a traditional streaming site** (like Netflix) where people only sit and watch.

It is an **interactive entertainment platform for African pop culture**. The core experience is built around active participation:
* Users don’t just watch a show—they **vote** for contestants, **predict** outcomes, play **trivia and games**, **tip and support creators**, and **earn reward points** while they watch.

---

### 2. Who are the main users?
There are 4 main types of users to design for:
1. **General Viewers**: People browsing and watching movies, comedy skits, and shows.
2. **Active Fans & Voters**: People who jump in to vote for reality show contestants, make sports predictions, play trivia, and climb leaderboards.
3. **Subscribers & Buyers**: People purchasing monthly subscriptions, renting movie premieres, or buying vote packs using the built-in digital wallet.
4. **Creators & Talents**: Content creators and entertainers who need simple screens to upload videos, view their fan support, and track earnings.

---

### 3. What are the core features users need to access?
The main focus areas for your designs should be:
* **Home & Content Discovery**: A clean homepage featuring trending shows, "continue watching," category filters, and quick search.
* **The Content Hubs ("Universes")**:
  * **Reality TV**: Show pages, contestant profiles, and live voting screens.
  * **Movies & Cinemaxis**: Movie listings, trailers, and rental/ticket passes.
  * **Comedy**: Stand-up specials and short, shareable viral clips.
  * **Games & Trivia**: Quick daily trivia battles and arcade leaderboards.
  * **Sports**: Match fixtures, highlights, and prediction games.
  * **Lifestyle**: Nightlife, event tickets, and city guides.
* **Interactive Video Player**: A video screen that lets users watch while easily accessing live chat, voting buttons, or tipping.
* **Wallet & Payments**: A simple, trustworthy screen where users can see their balance, add money, and view receipts.
* **Rewards & Streaks**: A visual rewards area showing daily login streaks, badges, and points earned that can be redeemed.
* **Creator Portal**: A clean, dedicated area for creators to upload videos and check their views and earnings.

---

### 4. Do all 162 pages need to be designed right now?
**No. You do not need to tackle all 162 pages at once.**

While the website's structure already maps out 162 pages, the project follows a phased rollout:
* **Phase 1 (Current Priority Focus)**:
  * Authentication & Onboarding (Login, Register, Interest picker)
  * Homepage & Main Navigation
  * Video Player & Watch experience
  * Reality TV (Show hub, Contestant profile, Voting flow)
  * Digital Wallet & Checkout screens
  * Rewards / Daily Streak screens
  * Creator Onboarding & Upload flow
* **Phase 2 & 3 (Later Focus)**:
  * Trivia games, sports prediction boards, deep fantasy storyworld pages, and advanced admin settings.

> **Takeaway**: Focus your design energy on creating a polished, reusable component system (cards, buttons, banners, popups) and the **Phase 1 core user flows**. The remaining sub-pages will naturally inherit that design system later.

---

### 5. What should be the main user journey on the website?
Rather than forcing users to pay or sign up immediately, the flow should be:

$$\text{Discover} \longrightarrow \text{Watch} \longrightarrow \text{Participate} \longrightarrow \text{Pay} \longrightarrow \text{Get Rewarded} \longrightarrow \text{Return}$$

1. **Discover (No immediate wall)**: The user visits the site, sees exciting trailers, trending shows, and live events without being blocked by an immediate forced login.
2. **Quick Sign-Up**: When they want to save a video or take an action, they get a fast, simple sign-up (Email, Google, or SMS code).
3. **Watch**: They start streaming a show or comedy clip in a clean player.
4. **Participate**: While watching, a clear, exciting prompt invites them to take part (*"Vote for your favorite contestant"* or *"Predict the winner"*).
5. **Pay (Low Friction)**: If they need votes or a pass, they make a quick payment or top up their digital wallet with clear pricing and confirmation.
6. **Reward & Return**: They get instant feedback (points earned, streak updated, badge unlocked) and friendly reminders to check back for results.

---

### 6. Should we use the current development website as the reference?
**Yes, but with clear roles for both:**

* **Use the current website as your functional guide**: It shows you what buttons need to exist, how screens connect to each other, and what information needs to be on each page.
* **Use the client briefing/design docs as your creative guide**: The current development site is mostly a basic functional scaffold. Your job is to transform it into a **modern, premium, mobile-friendly, and visually engaging experience** with great visual hierarchy, clear buttons, and polished dark/cinematic styling.
