PART I - AUTHORITY, MISSION & PRODUCT CONSTITUTION

## 0. AUTHORITY STATEMENT

This document establishes the controlling product-development and deployment architecture for Azeebo+.

It governs product strategy, platform architecture, MVP definition, release sequencing, universe activation, feature prioritisation, product governance, engineering boundaries, content ingestion, monetisation readiness, analytics, experimentation, reliability, security, operating responsibility, commercial readiness, launch readiness, capital release, and post-launch optimisation.

Where a lower-order product, engineering, design, universe, content or operations document conflicts with this authority, this document prevails unless formally superseded through an approved change-control process.

This authority is designed to prevent five forms of strategic failure:

- Building too much before proving demand.

- Rebuilding the same platform capability separately for different universes.

- Releasing universes without adequate content supply.

- Scaling user acquisition before economics are understood.

- Allowing product complexity to grow faster than organisational capability.

## 1. PRODUCT MISSION

Azeebo+ is being built as:

### AFRICA'S PARTICIPATORY ENTERTAINMENT OPERATING SYSTEM

It is not designed merely as a streaming service, creator application, gaming application, reality-show application, social network, or movie catalogue.

It is a shared digital infrastructure through which audiences can progressively:

### DISCOVER -\> WATCH -\> EXPLORE -\> PARTICIPATE -\> CREATE -\> PLAY -\> REWARD -\> TRANSACT -\> BELONG -\> RETURN.

## 2. PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT THESIS

Azeebo+ shall be developed according to one controlling principle:

> Build reusable engines once, validate them with real behaviour, and progressively activate the consumer universes that create the highest justified return on the next unit of capital.

The product shall therefore be developed in three distinct layers:

- Layer One - Core Platform: infrastructure used by most or all universes.

- Layer Two - Reusable Experience Engines: shared capabilities such as Participation, Rewards, Storyworld, Creator, Reality Show Factory and Games.

- Layer Three - Consumer Universes: the experiences ultimately visible to audiences.

## 3. THE PRODUCT CONSTITUTION

The following laws are controlling unless explicitly amended by the Platform Steering Committee and Board where required.

### LAW 01 - ONE PLATFORM IDENTITY

No universe may create a separate user-account system where the shared Azeebo+ identity can reasonably serve the requirement.

### LAW 02 - ONE USER, MANY EXPERIENCES

A user's history, preferences, entitlements, rewards and participation should accumulate across the ecosystem rather than reset at universe boundaries.

### LAW 03 - AOU IS IP, NOT AN AZEEBO+ UNIVERSE

Agbala Olodumare Universe is proprietary intellectual property. It initially manifests through Vertical, Movies & Series, Storyworld, Participation, and later other appropriate Azeebo+ surfaces.

### LAW 04 - NO EMPTY UNIVERSE

A consumer-facing universe shall not be publicly activated merely because its navigation design exists. It requires sufficient content, operations, commercial logic, support, analytics and user value.

### LAW 05 - BUILD ONCE BEFORE REBUILDING

Before any team creates a new service or feature, it must first determine whether an existing engine can satisfy the requirement.

### LAW 06 - PARTICIPATION IS PLATFORM INFRASTRUCTURE

Polls, quizzes, predictions, challenges, reactions and missions shall be reusable primitives rather than bespoke implementations for individual programmes.

### LAW 07 - MONEY IS NOT POINTS

The platform must maintain explicit separation between real monetary value, payments, content entitlements, loyalty points, promotional rewards, and future regulated gaming value.

### LAW 08 - NO FEATURE WITHOUT INSTRUMENTATION

Any material feature released to users must generate the analytics necessary to determine whether it creates value.

### LAW 09 - NO SCALE WITHOUT EVIDENCE

Major acquisition, content or engineering expenditure follows validated creative, technical, audience, platform, commercial and economic evidence.

### LAW 10 - NO SINGLE CRITICAL AI-VENDOR DEPENDENCY

VCF's AI-native production pipeline and Azeebo+'s production integrations shall remain model-agnostic wherever commercially practical.

### LAW 11 - AI MAY CREATE FICTION; AI MUST NOT CORRUPT FACT

Reality events must not be fabricated. Sports results must not be fabricated. Documentary/factual claims must not be synthetically altered in a way that misrepresents reality.

### LAW 12 - HUMAN RIGHTS AND CONSENT GOVERN DIGITAL PERFORMANCE

No actor, presenter, creator or performer shall have voice, likeness or digital performance replicated outside explicitly authorised contractual boundaries.

### LAW 13 - RIGHTS-COMPLETE BEFORE RELEASE

No commercial media asset enters public distribution without adequate rights, provenance and release status.

### LAW 14 - PRODUCT COMPLEXITY MUST EARN ITS EXISTENCE

Features that do not create measurable retention, engagement, revenue, operating efficiency or strategic advantage shall be optimised, paused or retired.

### LAW 15 - THE CUSTOMER SEES SIMPLICITY

The underlying system may be complex. The user experience must not be.

### LAW 16 - SUPPLY BEFORE SHELL

The platform shall not build elaborate universe interfaces significantly ahead of content supply.

### LAW 17 - OPERATIONS ARE PART OF PRODUCT

A feature is not complete until support, monitoring, permissions, failure recovery, analytics, and operating ownership exist.

### LAW 18 - REUSE IS A KPI

Platform reuse between titles and universes shall be measured.

### LAW 19 - CAPITAL IS LEARNING CAPITAL

Every significant development investment must identify what hypothesis is being tested, what evidence will be generated, and what subsequent decision that evidence enables.

### LAW 20 - TIME DOES NOT OVERRIDE A FAILED GATE

A calendar deadline shall not automatically authorise public launch, universe activation, or capital escalation where mandatory evidence gates have failed.

PART II - MVP RELEASE ARCHITECTURE & FOUNDING EXPERIENCE

## 8. THE FOUR MVP RELEASE STATES

The word MVP shall no longer represent one undifferentiated release.

Azeebo+ shall use four formal founding release stages:

- M0 - Experience Proof.

- M1 - Founding Closed Beta.

- M2 - Founding Public MVP.

- M3 - Monetisation & Retention Release.

## 9. M0 - EXPERIENCE PROOF

### Objective

Determine whether the Azeebo+ proposition is understandable and compelling before major production engineering is completed.

### Product state

Clickable/functional prototype. No broad public launch.

### Required flows

Home. Vertical. Long-form. AOU Hub. Character page. Timeline. Poll. Quiz. Rewards. Profile.

### Questions answered

Can users understand the product? Do they understand the relationship between Vertical and Series? Does Storyworld create curiosity? Is Participate intuitive? Does Rewards feel motivating rather than distracting?

### Exit gate

Experience architecture approved for M1 engineering.

## 10. M1 - FOUNDING CLOSED BETA

### Objective

Prove core user behaviour and product reliability.

### Mandatory capabilities

Identity. Onboarding. Home. Vertical playback. Long-form playback. Continue watching. AOU Hub Lite. Character pages. Basic timeline. Participation Lite. Analytics. Notifications Lite. CMS. Rights metadata. Admin. Feature flags. Crash/error reporting.

### Deliberately optional

Consumer payments. Advanced rewards. Sponsor inventory. Deep personalisation.

### Primary purpose

PROVE BEHAVIOUR.

## 11. M2 - FOUNDING PUBLIC MVP

M2 converts the working beta into a commercially viable public product.

### Adds

Payment-provider integration. Premium entitlement. Purchase history. Rewards Lite. Sponsor campaign configuration. Customer support tools. Production-grade security. Release management. Commercial analytics. Refund/reconciliation workflows. Operational dashboards.

### Primary purpose

PROVE MARKET.

## 12. M3 - MONETISATION & RETENTION RELEASE

M3 shall not be predetermined in full. It is built from actual M2 evidence.

Potential additions include pricing experiments, premium bundles, stronger recommendations, expanded missions, deeper Storyworld features, advanced notifications, better acquisition attribution, commercial segmentation and sponsor personalisation.

### Primary purpose

PROVE ECONOMICS.

## 30. PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

Final thresholds shall be validated during engineering benchmark, but every critical journey must have defined P50 latency, P95 latency, P99 latency where appropriate, playback startup time, buffer rate, API response time, page load, search response, payment response and entitlement propagation.

No public launch shall occur without documented performance baselines.

## 31. RELIABILITY & SRE DOCTRINE

Critical services require health monitoring, alert thresholds, runbooks, on-call ownership, rollback, backup, recovery procedure, dependency maps and capacity monitoring.

Every Severity-1 incident receives an incident commander, timeline, customer-impact assessment, root-cause analysis, remediation and postmortem.

## 32. RECOVERY OBJECTIVES

Every critical system must receive:

- RTO - Recovery Time Objective.

- RPO - Recovery Point Objective.

- Backup frequency.

- Restore-test cadence.

- Disaster-recovery owner.

These must be formalised before M2.

## 33. BANDWIDTH DOCTRINE

Azeebo+ shall be designed for variable network conditions.

Required behaviours include Adaptive bitrate, Selectable quality, Data Saver, Network-aware preloading, Graceful playback retry, Resume after interruption, Thumbnail optimisation, Image compression, Low-bandwidth error states, and downloads later where rights permit.

## 34. DEGRADED-NETWORK BEHAVIOUR

The product specification shall explicitly define user experience under severely degraded connection, 3G-quality connection, standard mobile broadband and high-speed Wi-Fi/5G.

For each, define playback quality, buffer strategy, preload amount, image quality, retry, offline messaging and payment behaviour.

## 35. PAYMENT INTERRUPTION BEHAVIOUR

If a payment process loses network connectivity, the platform must not silently charge twice; the transaction must receive a definitive state; the user must be informed; the entitlement must not be granted twice; and reconciliation must resolve uncertain status.

Payment retry logic must use idempotency controls.

- D0 - Editorial.

- D1 - Popularity + recency.

- D2 - Declared interests.

- D3 - Behavioural cohorts.

PART V - EXPERIMENTATION, RELEASE MANAGEMENT & FAILURE PLAYBOOKS

## 58. EXPERIMENTATION GOVERNANCE

All material experiments shall be registered.

Every experiment record includes Hypothesis, Feature, Target population, Primary metric, Guardrail metrics, Duration, Sample methodology, Stop condition, Privacy impact, Commercial impact, Decision owner and Outcome.

## 59. HIGH-RISK EXPERIMENTS

Additional approval is required for experiments involving pricing, payment, notifications, minors, reward economics, synthetic-media representation, premium access, sponsor commitments, regulated gaming or high-frequency user messaging.

No growth experiment may bypass consumer-protection or rights obligations.

## 60. FEATURE RETIREMENT DOCTRINE

A feature may be removed when usage remains negligible, maintenance exceeds value, user research identifies confusion, another capability supersedes it, economic value is negative, or risk becomes disproportionate.

Removing a bad feature is considered successful product management rather than failure.

## 61. FEATURE FLAGS

Every major new capability or universe should support controlled rollout.

Required use cases: internal only, staff beta, percentage rollout, cohort rollout, territory rollout, sponsor-specific rollout and emergency kill switch.

No high-risk feature should require a full app-store release merely to disable it.

## 62. RELEASE MANAGEMENT

Every production release requires release owner, approved build, QA result, security checks, analytics validation, rollback plan, release notes, support briefing and monitoring plan.

Critical releases receive formal launch command.

## 63. SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION

### SEV-1

Major platform outage. Payment charged with systematic entitlement failure. Critical data/security incident. Corrupted public result/vote. Large-scale rights violation.

### SEV-2

Major feature degraded. Significant playback failure. Partial payment issue.

### SEV-3

Material but localised user problem.

### SEV-4

Minor defect.

Each severity receives defined escalation and response expectations.

## 64. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - PAYMENT PROVIDER OUTAGE

Immediate actions:

- Disable affected purchase flow if necessary.

- Preserve user state.

- Display transparent status.

- Prevent duplicate transactions.

- Activate alternate provider where available.

- Reconcile outstanding payments.

- Inform Support and Finance.

After recovery, reconcile every uncertain transaction.

## 65. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - CDN / MEDIA OUTAGE

Actions:

- Identify dependency.

- Switch delivery path where architecture supports it.

- Degrade gracefully.

- Surface clear user messaging.

- Pause acquisition campaigns if playback is materially unavailable.

- Preserve progress.

Postmortem required for material outages.

## 66. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - PREMIUM ENTITLEMENT ERROR

If payment succeeds but access fails, the incident escalates immediately; the user receives access or refund remediation; the affected cohort is identified; root cause is contained; and Finance reconciles all affected transactions.

## 67. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - CONTENT RIGHTS CHALLENGE

Immediately identify title/asset, assess legal severity, geo-disable or remove where required, preserve master, notify rights authority, trace provenance and correct or replace affected asset.

## 68. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - AI LIKENESS / VOICE DISPUTE

Immediately suspend disputed asset where necessary, retrieve VTM permissions, retrieve provenance ledger, escalate to Legal and block derivative reuse until cleared.

## 69. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - APP STORE REJECTION

Maintain pre-submission compliance review, rejection owner, fallback web route where appropriate, release schedule contingency and SKU-specific documentation.

Critical launches shall not depend on a single untested last-minute store submission.

## 70. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - DATA INCIDENT

Actions: contain, preserve evidence, assess scope, engage security/legal, follow applicable notification obligations, protect affected users and complete root-cause remediation.

## 71. FAILURE PLAYBOOK - REALITY RESULT DISPUTE

Later Reality operations require an immutable vote ledger, timestamped audit trail, published rules, independent reconciliation, result freeze before publication and defined challenge/appeal protocol.
