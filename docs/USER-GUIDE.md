# Azeebo+ User Guide

> Get started with Azeebo+, the digital entertainment platform for multiple content universes.

---

## Getting Started

### Download

- **Web**: Visit `azeeboplus.com`
- **iOS**: App Store
- **Android**: Google Play

### Create an Account

1. Tap **Sign Up** and enter your email and password
2. Verify your email via the confirmation link
3. Complete your profile with a display name and picture

Google and Apple sign-in are also available.

---

## Browsing Content

### Universes

Content is organized into universes: **Reality**, **Comedy**, **Movies**, **Games**, **Sports**, **Lifestyle**, and **Vertical** (storyworlds). Tap any universe from the home screen to explore.

### Search

Use the search bar to find content across all universes. Filter by title, creator, category, or browse trending content.

### Watchlist

Tap the **bookmark icon** on any content to save it for later. Access your full watchlist from your profile or the main menu.

---

## Reality Voting

### How to Vote

1. Open a **Reality** show and select an **episode**
2. Pick a **contestant** and choose a vote quantity
3. Confirm -- cost is deducted from your wallet instantly
4. Results appear on the show's **leaderboard**

### Supervotes

Premium votes that carry extra leaderboard weight. Available for purchase from the Reality pricing page.

### Predictions

Before each episode, submit predictions about what will happen. Correct predictions earn reward points and wallet credit.

---

## Wallet & Payments

Your wallet holds your balance for voting, purchases, and transactions. Currency is NGN.

### Fund Wallet

1. Go to **Wallet** > **Fund Wallet**
2. Enter the amount and complete payment via Paystack or Flutterwave (card, bank transfer, USSD)

### Withdrawals

1. Go to **Wallet** > **Withdraw**
2. Enter your bank details and amount
3. Confirm -- processed within 1-3 business days

### Transaction History

View all past transactions from **Wallet** > **Transaction History** with date, description, amount, and status.

---

## Rewards & Points

### Earning Points

Earn points by completing daily missions, winning game tournaments, making correct predictions, referring users, and maintaining login streaks.

### Missions

Open **Rewards** to see available tasks. Each has a description and point value. Complete the action to claim your reward.

### Badges

Earn badges for milestones: first vote, prediction streaks, tipping creators, login streaks, and more. View your collection from your profile.

### Redemption

Redeem points for wallet credit, supervotes, or premium content access.

---

## Creator Portal

### Becoming a Creator

1. Open the Creator Portal (creator.azeeboplus.com) or enable creator mode in your profile
2. Complete onboarding with channel name, bio, and picture
3. Submit verification documents and wait for approval (1-3 business days)

### Uploading Content

1. Tap **Upload New Content** in the dashboard
2. Upload a file (MP4, MOV, WebM) and add title, description, and tags
3. Set visibility (public, subscribers only, or private)
4. Submit for moderation review -- content goes live after approval

### Analytics and Earnings

Your dashboard shows views, watch time, engagement, subscribers, and revenue. Earn from ad revenue, tips, subscriptions, and challenge prizes. Payouts go to your linked bank account.

## Settings & Privacy

- **Account**: Update display name, email, password, and linked accounts
- **Notifications**: Toggle push, email, and SMS notifications
- **Privacy**: Control profile visibility, watchlist visibility, data export, and account deletion
- **Security**: Enable MFA via an authenticator app in Settings > Security

---

## Troubleshooting

**Cannot log in** -- Check your credentials, use Forgot Password, or verify your email. Contact support if your account is suspended.

**Wallet not updating** -- Refresh the app, wait a few minutes after funding, or check transaction history. Contact support with your transaction reference.

**Content not loading** -- Check your internet connection, try switching networks, clear app cache, or update the app.

**Vote failed** -- Ensure sufficient balance, confirm voting window is open, and avoid rapid duplicate submissions.

**Upload rejected** -- Confirm your account is verified, file format is supported, and file size is within limits. Review moderation feedback and resubmit.

**Contact support** -- Go to Settings > Help & Support to open a ticket. Include your email and a description of the issue.
