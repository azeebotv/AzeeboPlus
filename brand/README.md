# Azeebo+ Brand & Design System Hub

Welcome to the central **Azeebo+ Brand and Design System** portal. This directory contains the official brand assets, extracted color palettes, Vanta Black OLED dark mode specifications, and full architectural overhaul documentation for the entire Azeebo+ platform.

---

## 🌟 Brand Identity Overview

- **Brand Mark:** Flame-gradient lowercase logotype with circular dynamic energy swirl & spark symbol (`azeebo+`).
- **Core Aesthetic:** **Vanta Black Luxury Dark Mode** (`#050505`) paired with warm **Sunset Flame Gradients** (`#E5251C` → `#F3501A` → `#FFA02E`), frosted obsidian glassmorphism, and neon universe accents.
- **Target Audience:** Modern global audiences consuming premium African entertainment, creator videos, live events, reality shows, movies, and interactive voting experiences.
